# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme operates on a structured yet iterative project lifecycle grounded in five core principles: **customer-first focus**, **iterative delivery** of small increments, **clear ownership** through defined roles, **data-informed decision-making**, and **psychological safety**. The organization divides project work into five distinct phases—Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective—each with specific deliverables and decision gates.

This documentation provides a complete guide to OctoAcme's project management methodology, covering key workflows, personas, communication strategies, and quality assurance practices. Communication flows through a structured cadence: weekly PM-to-PdM syncs, twice-weekly delivery standups, and monthly stakeholder updates, with ad-hoc escalations for critical blockers. By following these processes, teams ensure that work is validated early, thoroughly planned before execution begins, and continuously improved through post-release retrospectives.

## Process Guides by Phase

### 1. Project Initiation
[**Project Initiation Guide**](./octoacme-project-initiation.md)

Define the initial steps to validate business need and authorize work. Confirm measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and create a lightweight plan. Key deliverable: Project One-pager.

### 2. Project Planning
[**Project Planning Guide**](./octoacme-project-planning.md)

Turn an approved initiative into an actionable plan and backlog for delivery. Break work into shippable increments, identify dependencies and risks, and align timelines with responsibilities.

### 3. Execution & Tracking
[**Execution & Tracking Guide**](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)

Manage day-to-day execution and track progress toward project milestones. Coordinate through daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and GitHub Projects. Emphasis on small PRs, quality assurance, and blocker escalation.

### 4. Risk Management & Communication
[**Risk Management & Communication Guide**](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md)

Identify, assess, and manage risks throughout the project lifecycle. Maintain a Risk Register, communicate status to stakeholders, and follow escalation paths for dependencies and blockers.

### 5. Release & Deployment
[**Release & Deployment Guide**](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)

Standardize how OctoAcme releases features to production to reduce risk and improve observability. Includes pre-release requirements, deployment checklist, rollback procedures, and incident playbook.

### 6. Retrospectives & Continuous Improvement
[**Retrospective & Continuous Improvement Guide**](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements. Conduct retrospectives after sprints, releases, or milestones. Track action items and measure impact of improvements.

## Reference

### Roles & Personas
[**OctoAcme Personas**](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)

Understand the typical roles and responsibilities in OctoAcme projects:
- **Developers** – Design, build, test, and deliver software components
- **Product Managers** – Define what to build and measure outcomes
- **Project Managers** – Coordinate delivery, manage schedules, risks, and communications

## Key Artifacts

- **Project Charter / One-pager** – Captures problem, goal, success metrics, and stakeholders
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** – Defines phased delivery and milestones
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** – Prioritized work items with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** – Tracks risks, likelihood, impact, and mitigations
- **Retrospective Notes & Action Items** – Captures learnings and improvements

## Communication Cadence

- **Weekly PM-PdM sync** – Alignment on priorities and decisions
- **Twice-weekly delivery standups** – Progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** – Status reports and milestone reviews
- **Ad-hoc escalations** – For critical issues and blockers
